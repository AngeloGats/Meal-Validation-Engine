Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for financial assistance? +

Click on "New Application" or "Apply for Aid" in the navigation. Fill out the 3-step form with your personal information, financial details, and upload required documents.

What documents do I need? +

You'll need a clinical abstract from your hospital and a billing statement. These should clearly show your diagnosis and itemized charges.

How long does approval take? +

Most applications are reviewed within 5-7 business days. You'll receive email notifications at each stage of the review process.

What government aid programs are available? +

We work with DSWD, LINGAP, PhilHealth, and PCSO. You can apply for multiple programs simultaneously to maximize your financial assistance.

Important Information

All information is kept confidential and secure. Your privacy is our priority. For questions, contact our support team at support@fastcare.ph or call 1-800-FASTCARE.
